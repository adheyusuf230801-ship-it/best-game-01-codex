Bloomkeeper is a moonlit garden-defense game. Guide the keeper through a glowing garden, throwing dew bolts at hungry moths before they devour its blooms. Gather the nectar dropped by defeated moths to grow new blooms and bring the garden back to life.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, and hold the left mouse button or Space to fire. Hold Shift to dash, press P to pause and M to mute. Click or press Enter to begin; press Enter or R to restart after a run. Survive eight waves with only three hearts to see the garden flourish.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser

# or hand it in (writes out/dossier.json):
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
